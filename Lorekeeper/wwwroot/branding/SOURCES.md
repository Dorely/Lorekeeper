# Lorekeeper branding provenance

The Lorekeeper book-mark SVG and the application icons derived from it are
first-party assets owned by Dorely. The maintainer confirmed ownership of the
original and generated branding on 2026-10-05. The source SVG is `icon.svg`;
the adjacent PNG and ICO files are its application and packaging variants.

These assets are covered by the first-party license alternatives in the root
`LICENSE`. They are not third-party stock artwork. No separate trademark license
